## The project

A genuinely playable 2–8 player social deduction game, and an encrypted personal
safe, in one app. I built both layers.

## What I learned

The encryption has to be the security, not the disguise. A vault that relies on
nobody recognising the app is only private until the app becomes known, so the
threat model has to assume the attacker knows exactly what they are looking at.
